# RouterMonitor direction

Reference: the owner's RouterMonitor screenshot, interpreted as a compact read-only network console, not a landing page.

Reading this as: a home-network troubleshooting console for its owner, in a dense instrument-panel visual language, ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Charcoal surfaces reduce glare during continuous monitoring; muted orange identifies controls and selected states without competing with measurements.
- System sans-serif keeps labels readable without downloading fonts; monospace is limited to addresses and numeric data for scanning.
- A narrow connection strip precedes actual device and optical measurements, then client inventory and interface details. No promotional hero or scaffold sidebar.
- Tables carry client and interface records because comparison matters more than decorative cards. Small definition lists carry device properties.
- Owner-approved update: Apple-style Liquid Glass via @zakisheriff/liquid-glass. Glass is concentrated in the control layer and connection sheet; instrument data remains on opaque surfaces for readability.
- A low-contrast slate/warm gradient supplies color variation behind refractive controls. The sticky toolbar also overlays real scrolling content. Library-controlled rounded lenses signal interactive controls, not data cards.
- Four-pixel data-surface radii keep dense records distinct. No stock illustration, invented logo, or decorative icons.
- Spacing separates data sources, not arbitrary equal cards. Source failures stay visible without removing valid data from other sources.
- Responsive tables scroll within their own container; controls meet 44px minimum targets. Motion is restricted to control feedback.
- All displayed telemetry comes from authenticated router responses. Traffic rates require two counter samples and are not a speed test.
